## How to Read a Tree

- Level 1 is the start.
- Level n is the nth term of the sequence.
- Nodes at each level show the branching that generates the term value.
- Cumulative nodes correspond to the sequence term at that level.
- The ratio shown is nodes(level n) / nodes(level n-1). This is the term-to-term ratio of the sequence.

The limit of this ratio is the LCR. The early levels shown here are transient. At level 5 the ratio is 2.9362, which is approaching the modified-1 LCR of 3.037 but has not settled yet.

## Notes

- All three images show the same tree at different levels.
- The trees are exploratory. Whether they have a formal classification as a fractal, an L-system, or a graph attractor is an open question. See EOA Part 0, Question 11.1.
